An independent visualization of JAXA’s pre-launch planning trajectory from JPL Horizons target −239, not a flight reconstruction.

{{ABOUT_CONTENT}}

The sky uses 9,827 Hipparcos stars, magnitude-scaled white markers, bright-star names and constellation outlines. Its RA/Dec grid is spaced at 1 hour and 15°. Catalog directions are an orientation reference without proper-motion correction, not a precision observing chart; the stars remain at infinity when the camera pans.

All times are UTC. Body positions and radii are to scale; point markers are enlarged for visibility.
