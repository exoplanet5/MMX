MMX Earth Departure

JAXA’s Martian Moons eXploration mission is designed to investigate Phobos and Deimos and return samples from Phobos. This independent study shows only the initial Earth-departure arc in the specified six-day window.

Pre-launch planning ephemeris. JAXA trajectory 20261019_20281231_MMX_V0.2, provided through JPL Horizons target −239. No flight reconstruction, covariance or uncertainty envelope is supplied.

Reference frame and time

Geometric Earth-center states in ICRF equatorial axes, aligned closely with J2000. No light-time or aberration correction. All displayed epochs are UTC. The Moon and Sun use simultaneous geometric DE440s states.

The scene maps (X,Y,Z) to (X,Z,−Y) in units of Earth’s radius; this is a right-handed display rotation, not a change to the reported coordinates. Only the Earth texture rotates, using precomputed Skyfield Earth-orientation quaternions. Polar motion is omitted.

Sampling and validation

Intermediate states use cubic Hermite interpolation of positions and velocities; speed is derived from the interpolant’s velocity. The interface never extrapolates outside the window. Small position residuals do not imply meter-level knowledge of the future flight.
